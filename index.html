01 THE QUESTION

What are the biggest barriers preventing young people from participating in sport?

This project looks at research from Wales and the wider UK, with a focus on teenagers and the reasons participation can fall as young people get older.

ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Research first.
Local voices matter.

This project investigates why young people may not participate in sport, using published research alongside planned local surveys and interviews. The starting prediction is that gender and lack of time may be important factors, but local research is needed to test that prediction.

The published sources have different age ranges, dates and sample groups. Their percentages are useful context, but they do not prove the same pattern applies to every young person or community.

Next step: compare these findings with responses from young people in your own school or area.

How to read these figures

Survey findings are not automatically comparable: samples, age ranges, wording and collection dates differ. Some results are self-reported, and a reported barrier does not necessarily mean it caused someone to stop participating. The site presents figures as recorded in the project notes; verify them against the linked reports before submitting your work.
